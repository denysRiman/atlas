# Company Policies

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week. At least two working days per week should normally be spent in the Vienna office.

Temporary remote work from another EU country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Employees must receive approval from their manager before working from another country.

## Expenses

Business travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days after the trip. Hotel expenses are reimbursed up to 180 EUR per night unless a higher amount was approved before booking.

Meal expenses are reimbursed according to the applicable company travel policy.

## AI Usage

Confidential customer data must not be submitted to public AI services.

Approved internal AI systems such as Atlas may process company information according to the configured access controls.

AI-generated output should be reviewed before it is used for customer-facing communication or important business decisions.
